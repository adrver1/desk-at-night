pixel = albedo × ( 1.00 · Emoon + 1.00 · Elamp )

The two E terms are baked irradiance maps. Light adds linearly, so the weights can change every frame for free. The lamp weight follows a first-order lag: τ = 90 ms heating, 250 ms cooling. The desk toy moves, so it is the one thing not baked: its balls mirror two panoramas Cycles rendered from the toy, and they cast live shadows by taking their own light out of each E term.
